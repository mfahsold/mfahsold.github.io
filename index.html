Software projects with a local-first bias: writing tools, media tooling and private inference — everything runs on your own machine.

Lixity — understand your manuscript

Lixity reads the patterns in a manuscript: sentence rhythm, vocabulary, dialogue and tense, with the passages behind every result. A local research workspace keeps sources, claims and writing decisions connected. Analysis runs on your computer; the read-only example needs no installation.

Try the example report · Three-minute first look · Source repository

Montage AI — local-first AI video editing

An open-source post-production assistant that edits video from its transcript: beat-synced assembly, Shorts reframing, audio polish such as ducking and isolation, and OTIO/EDL export for Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve. Everything runs on your own machine, optionally across a small Kubernetes cluster.

Project page · Source repository

Fluxibri — sovereign inference and application stack

Fluxibri keeps private AI work on your own hardware: one workstation or a small K3s fleet, with the trusted developer-agent loop separated from end-user applications. Operations stay reproducible, version-pinned and rollback-aware, so local models and services can be updated and undone without guesswork.

Private project, in development — no public site yet.
