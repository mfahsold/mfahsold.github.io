Lixity — understand your manuscript

Lixity reads the patterns in a manuscript: sentence rhythm, vocabulary, dialogue and tense, with the passages behind every result. A local research workspace keeps sources, claims and writing decisions connected. Analysis runs on your computer; the read-only example needs no installation.
